# Compliance Matrix for PulseWard Hospital Management System

## Introduction

The Compliance Matrix outlines the compliance requirements for the PulseWard Hospital Management System (HMS). It serves as a reference for ensuring that the system adheres to relevant regulations, standards, and best practices in healthcare management.

## Compliance Requirements

| Compliance Area          | Requirement Description                                              | Compliance Status | Notes                                |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| HIPAA                    | Ensure the protection of patient health information.                 | Compliant         | Regular audits scheduled.            |
| GDPR                     | Data protection and privacy for EU citizens.                         | Compliant         | Data processing agreements in place. |
| HITECH                   | Enhance the privacy and security protections for health information. | Compliant         | Implemented necessary safeguards.    |
| ISO 27001                | Information security management system standards.                    | In Progress       | Certification process initiated.     |
| PCI-DSS                  | Payment Card Industry Data Security Standards for billing.           | Not Compliant     | Action plan required.                |
| FDA Regulations          | Compliance for software as a medical device (SaMD).                  | Not Applicable    | No SaMD features implemented.        |
| Local Health Regulations | Adherence to state-specific healthcare regulations.                  | Compliant         | Regular updates to be monitored.     |

## Compliance Monitoring

- **Frequency of Audits**: Quarterly
- **Responsible Parties**: Compliance Officer, IT Security Team
- **Documentation**: All compliance-related documentation must be maintained and updated regularly.

## Conclusion

The Compliance Matrix is a living document that will be updated as new regulations emerge and as the PulseWard HMS evolves. Regular reviews and updates will ensure ongoing compliance and the protection of patient information.
