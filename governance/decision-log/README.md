# Decision Log for PulseWard Hospital Management System

This document serves as the decision log for the PulseWard Hospital Management System project. It captures key decisions made throughout the project's lifecycle, including the rationale behind each decision, the stakeholders involved, and any relevant context.

## Purpose

The purpose of maintaining a decision log is to ensure transparency and accountability in the decision-making process. It helps in tracking the evolution of the project and provides a reference for future decisions.

## Structure

Each entry in the decision log should include the following information:

- **Date**: The date the decision was made.
- **Decision**: A brief description of the decision.
- **Rationale**: The reasoning behind the decision.
- **Stakeholders**: Individuals or groups involved in the decision-making process.
- **Status**: The current status of the decision (e.g., implemented, pending, revisited).
- **Related Documents**: Links to any relevant documents or discussions.

## Maintenance

The decision log should be updated regularly, especially after key meetings or milestones. It is the responsibility of the project manager to ensure that the log is kept current and accessible to all stakeholders.

## Conclusion

Maintaining a decision log is crucial for the success of the PulseWard Hospital Management System project. It fosters a culture of transparency and informed decision-making, ultimately contributing to the project's overall effectiveness and efficiency.
