# On-Call Procedures for PulseWard Hospital Management System

## Overview

This document outlines the on-call procedures for the PulseWard Hospital Management System (HMS). It is essential for ensuring that critical issues are addressed promptly and efficiently, minimizing downtime and maintaining high-quality patient care.

## Purpose

The purpose of this on-call procedure is to provide clear guidelines for staff who are responsible for responding to incidents outside of regular working hours. This includes defining roles, responsibilities, and escalation paths.

## On-Call Schedule

- The on-call schedule will be maintained by the operations team.
- Each team member will be assigned a week of on-call duty.
- The schedule will be communicated via email and posted on the internal dashboard.

## Roles and Responsibilities

### On-Call Staff

- Respond to incidents reported during their on-call period.
- Document all incidents and actions taken in the incident management system.
- Escalate issues to senior staff or management as necessary.

### Incident Manager

- Oversee the incident response process.
- Ensure that all incidents are logged and tracked.
- Conduct post-incident reviews to identify areas for improvement.

## Incident Reporting

- All incidents should be reported through the designated incident management system.
- Include the following information in the report:
  - Date and time of the incident
  - Description of the issue
  - Impact on services
  - Actions taken

## Escalation Procedures

1. **Level 1**: On-call staff will attempt to resolve the issue.
2. **Level 2**: If unresolved, escalate to the Incident Manager.
3. **Level 3**: If the Incident Manager cannot resolve the issue, escalate to the senior management team.

## Communication

- Use the designated communication channels (e.g., Slack, email) for all incident-related communications.
- Ensure that all stakeholders are kept informed of the status of ongoing incidents.

## Documentation

- Maintain detailed records of all incidents, including resolutions and any follow-up actions required.
- Review and update this document annually or after significant incidents.

## Training

- All on-call staff must undergo training on the incident management system and procedures.
- Regular drills will be conducted to ensure readiness.

## Conclusion

Adhering to these on-call procedures will help ensure that the PulseWard HMS operates smoothly, providing continuous support to healthcare professionals and patients alike.
