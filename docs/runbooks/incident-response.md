# Incident Response Runbook for PulseWard Hospital Management System

## Purpose

The purpose of this incident response runbook is to provide a structured approach for identifying, managing, and resolving incidents that may affect the PulseWard Hospital Management System. This document outlines the procedures to follow in the event of an incident, ensuring that all team members are aware of their roles and responsibilities.

## Scope

This runbook applies to all incidents that may impact the availability, integrity, or confidentiality of the PulseWard Hospital Management System, including but not limited to security breaches, system outages, and data loss.

## Incident Response Team

- **Incident Manager**: Responsible for overseeing the incident response process.
- **Technical Lead**: Responsible for technical assessment and resolution of the incident.
- **Communications Lead**: Responsible for internal and external communications regarding the incident.
- **Support Staff**: Responsible for assisting in the resolution of the incident as needed.

## Incident Identification

1. **Monitoring**: Utilize monitoring tools to detect anomalies or alerts.
2. **Reporting**: Encourage team members to report any suspected incidents immediately.
3. **Initial Assessment**: The Incident Manager will perform an initial assessment to determine the severity and impact of the incident.

## Incident Classification

- **Critical**: Major impact on operations, requires immediate attention.
- **High**: Significant impact, but operations can continue with limited functionality.
- **Medium**: Moderate impact, can be addressed in a timely manner.
- **Low**: Minimal impact, can be scheduled for resolution.

## Incident Response Steps

1. **Containment**:

   - For critical incidents, isolate affected systems to prevent further damage.
   - Implement temporary workarounds if necessary.

2. **Eradication**:

   - Identify the root cause of the incident.
   - Remove any malicious components or vulnerabilities.

3. **Recovery**:

   - Restore affected systems to normal operation.
   - Validate that systems are functioning correctly and securely.

4. **Post-Incident Review**:
   - Conduct a review meeting with the incident response team.
   - Document lessons learned and update incident response procedures as necessary.

## Communication Plan

- **Internal Communication**: Notify all relevant stakeholders about the incident and provide regular updates.
- **External Communication**: If necessary, prepare a public statement to inform affected parties and the media.

## Documentation

- Maintain detailed records of the incident, including:
  - Timeline of events
  - Actions taken
  - Communications made
  - Lessons learned

## Tools and Resources

- Incident management software
- Monitoring tools
- Communication platforms (e.g., Slack, email)

## Conclusion

This incident response runbook serves as a guide for effectively managing incidents within the PulseWard Hospital Management System. Adhering to these procedures will help minimize the impact of incidents and ensure a swift recovery. Regular reviews and updates to this document will be conducted to adapt to new challenges and improve response capabilities.
