# Subtle Branding and Easy UX Guidelines

## Intent

Branding must feel professional, calm, and healthcare-grade while keeping workflows simple for staff.

## Visual Direction

- Keep contrast high and visual noise low.
- Use one primary color, one secondary, and one warm accent.
- Prefer clean icons and restrained gradients.
- Reserve bold accent for calls-to-action and alerts only.

## UI/UX Simplicity Rules

- Keep top-level navigation to 5 to 7 items.
- Keep critical actions one click away from dashboard widgets.
- Use plain language labels for non-technical users.
- Keep forms short; progressive disclosure for advanced options.
- Ensure all primary screens are mobile-usable.

## Tenant Branding Safety

- Hospital branding may change logos/colors only through admin configuration.
- Core layouts and interaction patterns remain consistent across tenants.
- Branding updates require accessibility check and audit log.

## Accessibility Baseline

- WCAG AA contrast minimum for text and buttons.
- Focus indicators always visible.
- Do not encode status by color alone.

## Asset Naming Convention

- `logo-light.svg`, `logo-dark.svg`, `favicon.ico`, `app-icon.png`
- Tenant folder format: `assets/tenants/<tenantKey>/branding/`
